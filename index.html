This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Gallery: Photo Cleaner handles information, accesses your media library, uses advertising and third-party services, and manages device permissions.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Smart Gallery: Photo Cleaner does not collect or store your personal photo and video content on its own servers. Photo and video analysis is performed directly on your device whenever possible. However, third-party services used by the app may process limited technical information for purposes such as advertising, purchase management, error detection, fraud prevention, analytics, and improving app performance.

  • Access to your photo and video library is used only to provide gallery cleaning, analysis, viewing, deletion, compression, and organization features.
  • Your photo and video files are not uploaded by Smart Gallery to advertising providers or Smart Gallery servers.
  • Smart Gallery does not store your name, email address, or media library contents on its own servers.
  • Third-party services may process limited technical information related to advertising, crash reporting, subscriptions, diagnostics, security, and performance measurement.
The contents of your photos and videos are not analyzed for advertising purposes or shared with advertising providers for ad targeting.

2. Photo and Video Library Access

Smart Gallery requests access to your photo and video library in order to analyze your gallery and help you clean and organize it.

  • Detect blurry, dark, overexposed, or other photos you may want to review.
  • Identify screenshots and duplicate or similar photos.
  • Organize videos by size or similarity.
  • Delete photos and videos that you select.
  • Compress supported videos and save the compressed version back to your photo library.
Photo and video analysis is performed on your device. Smart Gallery does not upload your media files to its own servers or advertising providers.

3. Photo Analysis and Face Detection

Smart Gallery may analyze photos for quality, similarity, and certain visual characteristics in order to help identify photos you may want to review. Supported versions of the app may also use on-device face detection to identify or group photos containing faces.

  • Blur detection.
  • Dark or overexposed photo detection.
  • Detection of certain quality issues such as closed eyes.
  • Screenshot detection.
  • Grouping duplicate or similar photos.
  • On-device detection of faces in supported features.
Photo analysis and face detection are performed on the device. Smart Gallery does not upload face images or biometric templates to its own servers or advertising providers. Automated results are suggestions only; you always decide whether a photo should be deleted.

4. Video Processing

Smart Gallery allows you to browse videos on your device, review them by size or similarity, and compress supported videos.

  • Video files are not uploaded to external servers by Smart Gallery.
  • Video compression is performed on your device.
  • A compressed video may be saved as a new media item in your photo library.
  • Deleting the original video requires user approval.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

Smart Gallery may offer optional paid features and a yearly Premium subscription. Purchases are processed through the Apple App Store. Smart Gallery does not directly collect or store your credit card or payment information.

  • Apple App Store processes payments.
  • RevenueCat helps manage subscription and Premium entitlement status.
  • RevenueCat may process technical information related to subscription status, products, purchase events, and entitlements.
RevenueCat privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

6. Crash Reports and Technical Data

Smart Gallery may use Sentry to improve application reliability and identify technical problems.

  • App version and operating system information.
  • Crash and error reports.
  • Technical information about the part of the app where an error occurred.
  • Limited diagnostic and performance information.
Your photo and video files are not intentionally included in crash reports.

7. Advertising and Google AdMob

Smart Gallery may display advertisements through Google AdMob in order to support free functionality within the app. The app may include banner advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements that provide certain in-app benefits after the advertisement has been completed.

  • Banner advertisements may appear in certain areas of the application.
  • Watching rewarded advertisements is optional.
  • After completing a rewarded advertisement, the app may provide credits to delete a certain number of photos or use other supported features.
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK may process technical information for advertising delivery, measurement, security, analytics, fraud prevention, and improving advertising performance.
  • This information may include IP address, approximate or general location derived from IP address, device or app identifiers, advertising data, advertisements viewed, app interactions, video views, crash information, diagnostic data, and performance information.
  • Smart Gallery does not send the content of your photos or videos to Google AdMob for advertising, personalization, or targeting purposes.
Advertising data is processed according to Google's own privacy policies and applicable privacy requirements.

8. Ad Personalization and Device Identifiers

How advertisements are delivered may depend on your device settings, your region, applicable privacy regulations, and the Google AdMob configuration used by Smart Gallery.

  • Where applicable, Google Mobile Ads SDK may process a device advertising identifier or other app- or developer-bounded identifiers.
  • You can use privacy and advertising controls provided by your operating system to limit certain advertising identifiers or tracking functionality.
  • Where required by applicable law or platform rules, Smart Gallery may request additional consent before enabling certain advertising functionality.
  • Advertising and privacy options may vary depending on your country or region.
Smart Gallery's photo and video analysis functionality operates independently from advertising personalization.

9. Third-Party Services

Smart Gallery may use the following third-party services to operate the app, display advertisements, manage purchases, and identify technical problems:

  • Apple App Store – payment processing and app distribution.
  • RevenueCat – subscription management and purchase entitlement management.
  • Sentry – crash, error, and diagnostic reporting.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK – banner and rewarded advertisement delivery, advertising measurement, analytics, performance measurement, security, and fraud prevention.
These third-party providers process data according to their own privacy policies and data processing practices.

10. Data Retention

Certain information related to photo and video analysis may be stored locally on your device to improve your experience. This may include recent scan results, scan cache information, deletion credits, or application preferences.

  • This information may be stored in Smart Gallery's local application storage.
  • Uninstalling the application may remove locally stored application data.
  • Original photos and videos stored in your media library remain separate from Smart Gallery's local application data.
  • Data processed by Google AdMob, RevenueCat, Sentry, or Apple is subject to the relevant provider's retention practices.

11. Permissions

Smart Gallery may request device permissions in order to provide certain functionality.

  • Photo and video library access – for media analysis, viewing, deletion, organization, compression, and saving compressed media.
  • Notification permission – for optional gallery cleaning or scan reminders.
  • Advertising or tracking permission, where applicable – when required by the operating system, applicable law, or advertising configuration.
You can change or disable permissions at any time through your device's system settings.

12. Children's Privacy

Smart Gallery is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The advertising functionality within Smart Gallery is also not designed to make the application a service directed toward children.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Smart Gallery functionality, third-party services, advertising practices, or legal requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page together with a revised "Last updated" date.

14. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: smartgalleryphoto@gmail.com

2. Fotoğraf ve Video Kütüphanesi Erişimi

Smart Gallery, galerinizi analiz etmek, düzenlemek ve temizlemenize yardımcı olmak için fotoğraf ve video kütüphanenize erişim ister.

  • Bulanık, karanlık, aşırı pozlanmış veya gözden geçirmek isteyebileceğiniz diğer fotoğrafları tespit etmek.
  • Ekran görüntülerini ve benzer ya da tekrar eden fotoğrafları belirlemek.
  • Videoları boyutlarına veya benzerliklerine göre düzenlemek.
  • Seçtiğiniz fotoğraf ve videoları silmek.
  • Desteklenen videoları sıkıştırmak ve sıkıştırılmış kopyayı fotoğraf kütüphanenize kaydetmek.
Fotoğraf ve video analizi cihazınızda gerçekleştirilir. Smart Gallery medya dosyalarınızı kendi sunucularına veya reklam sağlayıcılarına yüklemez.

3. Fotoğraf Analizi ve Yüz Tespiti

Smart Gallery, gözden geçirmek isteyebileceğiniz fotoğrafları belirlemeye yardımcı olmak amacıyla fotoğrafları kalite, benzerlik ve belirli görsel özellikler açısından analiz edebilir. Uygulamanın desteklenen sürümleri ayrıca yüz içeren fotoğrafları belirlemek veya gruplamak amacıyla cihaz üzerinde yüz tespiti kullanabilir.

  • Bulanıklık tespiti.
  • Karanlık veya aşırı pozlanmış fotoğraf tespiti.
  • Kapalı göz gibi belirli kalite sorunlarının tespiti.
  • Ekran görüntüsü tespiti.
  • Tekrar eden veya benzer fotoğrafların gruplandırılması.
  • Desteklenen özelliklerde cihaz üzerinde yüz tespiti.
Fotoğraf analizi ve yüz tespiti cihaz üzerinde gerçekleştirilir. Smart Gallery yüz görüntülerini veya biyometrik şablonları kendi sunucularına ya da reklam sağlayıcılarına yüklemez. Otomatik sonuçlar yalnızca öneridir; bir fotoğrafın silinip silinmeyeceğine her zaman siz karar verirsiniz.
